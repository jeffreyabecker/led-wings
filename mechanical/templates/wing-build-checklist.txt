------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ ] 2. Laminate + seal all cardboard pieces
[ ] 3. Assemble the root Y-brackets; mount both spars per wing at the
         plate's top corners (roots at +-20 cm, y = 0)

[ ] 5. Cut the feathers: packing-foam bodies + quill wires (list B)
[ ] 6. Dry-fit feather rows on the mockup: secondaries -> primaries -> coverts
[ ] 7. Mount the quills (+4 cm embed into the spars), bend wires for tip splay
[ ] 8. Fit the harness straps (over-shoulder + cross-chest)
[ ] 9. Fit the back-coverts panel
